## Key Data Points Not in Final Entry

- GPTs had three building blocks: Instructions, Expanded Knowledge (up to 20 files), Actions (API calls via OpenAPI specs)
- Altman built "Startup Mentor" GPT on stage in ~3 minutes, uploading a Y Combinator lecture
- Board crisis: Nov 17 fired, Nov 22 reinstated. Mira Murati interim CEO, then Emmett Shear, then Altman back. 700+ employees signed open letter.
- GPT Store categories: Top Picks, DALL-E, Writing, Productivity, Research & Analysis, Programming, Education, Lifestyle
- Featured GPTs: Consensus, Canva, Khan Academy Code Tutor, AllTrails
- Revenue sharing: restricted to invite-only US-based builders with 25+ conversations/week sustained
- Top creators earned hundreds to low thousands — "a pittance compared to App Store rhetoric"
- Gems: instructions up to 15,000 tokens, 10 uploaded files (vs GPTs' 20)
- Gems invokable from within Google Workspace side panel
- MCP donated to Linux Foundation AAIF in Dec 2025, co-founded by Anthropic, Block, and OpenAI
- Sam Altman on MCP: "People love MCP and we are excited to add support across our products" (Mar 2025)
