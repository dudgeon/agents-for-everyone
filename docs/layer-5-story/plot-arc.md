## Structural Constraint (from user)

- ~2/3 of the book: Skepticism arc — walking through AI eras with honest strengths and failures
- ~1/3 of the book: Conversion arc — Skeptic learns to build and align agentic systems

## Notes from Early Discussion

- Maven introduces agentic tool, Skeptic is unimpressed
- Skeptic takes Maven back through time (mechanic TBD — literal or metaphorical)
- Witty banter; Maven has to earn it
- Maven acknowledges real failures at each stage
- Skeptic's resistance gradually softens
- Culminates in understanding nurturing, alignment, CLAUDE.md, skills

## Thematic Threads (carry forward from all layers)

1. **Harness > Model**: The tooling around the model matters more than raw intelligence
2. **Trust is Earned**: The Skeptic's distrust is rational; it has to be overcome with evidence
3. **Alignment Through Investment**: Agentic tools get better the more you invest in shaping them
4. **The File System is the Interface**: Moving from chat bubbles to file-system-native work is a paradigm shift
5. **Failure is Informative**: Each era's failures point to what the next era needs to solve

## DRAFT — "After Credits" Scene

> **Status: Draft / Needs Refinement**

A third character enters — someone who's been on a 4-year internet-free retreat in the mountains.

**Character 3:** "What did I miss?"

**Maven:** "Well, there's this new AI thing called agents..."

Character 3 immediately starts ripping off a checklist — "what would need to be true for this to be interesting":

- Can I connect it to my own trustworthy data?
- Can I specify rules and trust it will follow them?
- Can it introspect, when prompted, and surface new ideas to improve its performance in the future?
- Can I assign it long-running tasks that combine research, synthesis, and creating PRDs, software specs, and arbitrary business documents?
- _(escalate to something weirdly specific — e.g., "can I teach it my writing voice and have it draft a 40-page grant proposal while I sleep?")_

**Maven and Skeptic, in unison:** "Yes!"

### Why this works
- Character 3's questions are the Layer 2 primitives rephrased as requirements — a recap for anyone who just read the book
- The rapid-fire delivery is comedic against two characters who lived through 200 pages of this
- Maven and Skeptic answering together shows their arcs converged
- The reader IS Character 3 — they came in not knowing, and now they can answer "yes" too

### Still needs
- Character 3's identity/voice — who is this person? Why were they offline?
- Exact question list (should map cleanly to the final primitives)
- The beat before the final "yes" — Maven and Skeptic sharing a look on the last, most ambitious question
- Whether this is literally a comic panel sequence or a prose epilogue
