## The Maven

**Name**: TBD
**Age**: TBD
**Background**: TBD

**Core trait**: Enthusiastic about agentic AI but intellectually honest. Not a hype person — genuinely believes in this because they've done the work to understand it.

**Voice notes**:
- Has to earn the Skeptic's trust — can't just assert things
- Acknowledges real problems with each era

## The Skeptic

**Name**: TBD
**Age**: TBD
**Background**: TBD

**Core trait**: AI-conversant, not a luddite. Has tried these tools. Was burned. Wrote them off based on real, specific experiences.

**Voice notes**:
- Skepticism is informed, not ignorant
- Has specific memories of failures ("Remember when it hallucinated that citation?")
- Gradually softens as cumulative progress becomes undeniable
- In Act 2, their skepticism transforms into careful, deliberate engagement

**Arc**: From informed skeptic → reluctant acknowledger → genuinely invested learner
